About Dream Cuts

Made with kids in mind

Our story

Fun for the kids, zero stress for Mum & Dad

Dream Cuts was established right here in Adelaide with your children in mind. We know a trip to the hairdresser can be a big deal for little ones, so we built a salon that feels like a playground — ride-in cars, bright colours, games and friendly faces.

From very first haircuts to enchanting braids and memorable birthday parties, we’re here for every milestone.

Sensory friendly

We welcome all children

We’re proud supporters of the Autism Friendly Charter. Our Sensory Cut gives extra time, care and patience for children who need a calmer, gentler visit.

  • Quiet hairdryers
  • Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5 and iPads
  • Fidget and sensory toys
  • Go-at-their-own-pace appointments

Good to know

Our salon policies

Head lice & nits

To keep everyone safe we can’t provide services to children with live lice or nits. If we spot them at the pre-check, we’ll happily reschedule.

Kindness first

We have a zero tolerance policy for abuse toward our staff, so the salon stays a happy place for everyone.
