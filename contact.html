    FAQ

    Questions parents ask

    Do I need to book?

    We recommend booking online so you get the time and stylist you want. Just tap Book Now to choose your service, stylist and time.

    What age groups do you cater for?

    We look after kids from their very first haircut right up to 16 years — plus Mum & Me packages for grown-ups.

    My child is anxious or has sensory needs. Can you help?

    Absolutely. Book a Sensory Cut for extra time, quiet hairdryers and sensory toys. We support the Autism Friendly Charter.

    Are there surcharges on weekends?

    Yes — 6% on Saturdays and 18% on Sundays.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    No charge with 24+ hours’ notice. 50% if cancelled 3 hours prior, and 100% within 3 hours, for no-shows, or if more than 10 minutes late.

    Do you sell gift vouchers?

    Yes! Gift vouchers can be used on all of our services — perfect for birthdays.
