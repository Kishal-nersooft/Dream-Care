Terms and conditions

Dream Cuts uses only high-quality products and takes all due care in performing these services. Dream Cuts cannot be held responsible for any adverse reactions which can occur as a result of the customer failing to inform our staff of a) any known allergies or b) reactions developed after a previous treatment/s.
